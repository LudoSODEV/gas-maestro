## AssoConnect

Exports XLSX spreadsheets. The spreadsheet has 1 sheet named "Export 1".
Two types of exports: "Structures" and "Personnes".
Table-like data format: unique headers, first column has unique IDs.

## Application Collecte

Exports XSLX spreadsheets.
Custom data format. Depends on type of data.

Lots of different exports:

 - Structures
 - Inscriptions (Plannings)
 - Groupes d'anonymes
 - Utilisateurs

May need to coalesce multiple data sources, e.g. Groupes + Structures, or Inscriptions from different campaigns related to the same event.
